## Abstract

This proposal explored activating extensions with a wildcard selected by receiver type. Its
implementation was reconsidered before release because a receiver wildcard could silently collect
same-receiver extensions from unrelated packages.

## Decision

Superseded 2026-10-19 by [ZEP-0034](./ZEP-0034.md). The `Type.*` receiver-family behavior is
withdrawn; wildcard imports are scoped to declaration namespaces instead.
