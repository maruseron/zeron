## Abstract

This proposal adds underscores as visual digit separators in numeric literals, allowing long
integer and fractional digit sequences to be grouped without changing their value or type.

## Motivation

Long numeric literals are difficult to scan and verify, particularly when they represent grouped
quantities, bit masks, or fixed-width values. Separators improve readability without adding new
numeric types or changing arithmetic semantics.

## Goals

- Permit readable digit grouping in supported integer and floating-point literal forms.
- Define separator placement and diagnostics consistently across decimal and radix-prefixed forms.
- Ensure separators do not affect literal value, inferred type, or runtime representation.

## Non-goals

- Adding new numeric types, literal suffixes, or radix syntax by itself.
- Introducing exponent notation or changing floating-point parsing.
- Allowing arbitrary punctuation or locale-dependent grouping.

## Proposal

Allow `_` between two valid digits belonging to the same numeric component. Examples include:

```text
1_000_000
12_345L
3.141_592
0xFF_FF
```

Separators are lexical only and are removed before numeric conversion. In decimal integers and
fractional literals, they may occur within the integer or fractional digit sequence. In radix-
prefixed integers, they may occur within the radix digit sequence. A separator may not lead or
trail a digit sequence, appear consecutively, or be adjacent to a radix prefix, decimal point, or
type suffix. Invalid placement produces a scanner diagnostic rather than silently changing token
boundaries.

Exponent notation is not currently supported and is outside this proposal. Its use with unit
literal suffixes is explored in [ZEP-0044](./ZEP-0044.md); separator placement within exponent
digits must be specified separately from the rules here. The formatter preserves the source
spelling and grouping of numeric literals rather than inserting or normalizing separators.

## Compatibility and impact

This is additive syntax for literals that currently parse successfully. Inputs that previously
produced a diagnostic may become valid when separators are used correctly. The scanner, formatter,
and syntax-highlighting grammar should agree on separator placement; formatting policy for inserting
or preserving separators remains outside this proposal.

## Alternatives

- **No separators** — avoids scanner changes, but leaves long literal values harder to inspect.
- **Allow separators anywhere** — maximizes flexibility but creates confusing and inconsistent
  spellings around prefixes, decimal points, and suffixes.
- **Use apostrophes or locale grouping** — less familiar for source literals and potentially
  ambiguous with existing string/character syntax or formatting conventions.

## Implementation and validation

The scanner validates separator placement before converting literal values. Tests cover valid
decimal and radix grouping, malformed separator placement, formatter preservation, and compiled
runtime values.

## Open questions

None for the accepted scope. Separators apply to the currently supported decimal integer and
fractional forms and to radix-prefixed integer forms defined by [ZEP-0040](./ZEP-0040.md).

## Decision

Accepted 2026-10-19. Permit underscores only between digits within the current decimal integer and
